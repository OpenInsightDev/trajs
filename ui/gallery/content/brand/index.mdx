title: The logo

The logo is trajs' own drawing — the one thing in `@trajs/ui-component` that is not a
mapping of `effect/ai`'s model onto shadcn/ui. It is one path held once: `Icon`
draws the mark alone, and `Logo` draws the mark with the wordmark beside it, so a
lockup cannot drift from the icon it is built on.

## The colourways

The mark has no colour of its own. Every path is `currentColor`, so a logo is
drawn in the colour of the text it sits in — which is what makes a colourway a
ground and a colour and nothing else. There are two it is built from: brand white
`#F6F6F6` and brand black `#171717`, each the other's inverse. The gold the
lockup is also used in, `#D4AF37`, is an accent: the same drawing, on either
ground, and never a third colour inside one lockup.

## What a class decides

How big the logo is drawn, how heavy the mark's lines are and what colour each half
of the lockup is are classes rather than props of the package's own: `h-*`/`w-*`
is the size, `stroke-*` the weight and the colour of the mark's lines, `fill-*` the
colour of the letters — `text-*` both — each of them a default in the markup that
the class takes over. The site's navbar is why the weight matters: at the ~24px a
navbar gives it, the weight the mark is drawn at is half a pixel, and the mark's
frame takes about 4.6 before the stroke runs out of it.

What a class cannot decide is the drawing: the paths, the weight they default to,
the gap between the mark and the letters, and the boxes they sit in — 92 × 146 for
the mark, 250 × 146 for the lockup, which is the mark's box extended to the right.

## The wordmark is set in IBM Plex Sans

The letters are IBM Plex Sans, set with the variable font at `wght 250` — an em of
45.93 units in the lockup's box, on a baseline at y 104.93, with +0.0802 em of
tracking — and held as paths rather than as live text because a logo is drawn
rather than typed: it is never rendered in a fallback face, it survives an exported
`.svg` and an Open Graph image, and no app has to load a font for it. Another
weight is set again in that font rather than redrawn, which makes it a generation
step and not a redesign.

The paths are the export rounded to two decimals with a command to a line, so a
stroke of the mark or a letter of the name can be found and moved without a vector
tool.

## The one surface that cannot be a component

A browser tab reads a file rather than a component, so the apps keep their own
`favicon.svg` in `public/`. Everywhere a page draws the logo, it draws these
components.
