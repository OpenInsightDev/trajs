---
title: ScrollArea
description: A region that scrolls without the browser's scrollbar.
---

import { ScrollArea } from "@trajs/shadcn/scroll-area";

A scroll area is a viewport and the scrollbar the components draw, rather than
the platform's own. That is what lets the bar be styled with the tokens, be
placed inside a rounded surface, and stay out of the way until the region is
scrolled.

<Preview>
  <ScrollArea className="h-40 w-full max-w-sm rounded-lg border">
    <div className="flex flex-col gap-2 p-3 text-sm">
      {Array.from({ length: 12 }, (_, index) => (
        <div key={index}>{`turn ${index + 1} — answered in one part`}</div>
      ))}
    </div>
  </ScrollArea>
</Preview>

The horizontal bar is a `ScrollBar` with `orientation="horizontal"`, which is
what a wide table inside a narrow surface is scrolled with.
