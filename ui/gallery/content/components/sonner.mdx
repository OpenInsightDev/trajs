A toast is raised by an event rather than by a page being read, which is why
this one is raised by pressing the buttons above. It is the answer an action
gets when it should not interrupt: importing a file, copying a part, a failure
that needs no decision. `Toaster` is the single surface the toasts are collected
on — a page mounts one — and each toast settles in a corner and leaves by
itself.

Success, error, warning, info and loading are stated on the call that raises the
toast, and each arrives with the icon the design language gives it. A drawer
that needs an answer is an [AlertDialog](/components/alert-dialog) instead: a
toast cannot be answered.
