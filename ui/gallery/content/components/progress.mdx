---
title: Progress
description: How far along a known amount of work is.
---

import { Progress, ProgressLabel, ProgressValue } from "@trajs/ui-shadcn/progress";

A progress bar is for work whose size is known, and the value is that size, not
a decoration: the track and the indicator are drawn from it, and `ProgressValue`
states it in figures for the reader who wants the number. `value={null}` is the
one that has no end yet.

<Preview>
  <div className="flex w-full max-w-sm flex-col gap-5">
    <Progress value={62}>
      <ProgressLabel>Importing</ProgressLabel>
      <ProgressValue />
    </Progress>
    <Progress value={100}>
      <ProgressLabel>Read</ProgressLabel>
      <ProgressValue />
    </Progress>
  </div>
</Preview>

Work of unknown length is a [Spinner](/components/spinner): a bar that cannot
say how far along it is says less than a spinner that admits it does not know.
