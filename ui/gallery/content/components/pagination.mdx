---
title: Pagination
description: The pages around this one, as links to them.
---

import {
  Pagination,
  PaginationContent,
  PaginationEllipsis,
  PaginationItem,
  PaginationLink,
  PaginationNext,
  PaginationPrevious,
} from "@trajs/ui-shadcn/pagination";

Pagination is links, not buttons: a page is somewhere a reader can go, and the
item that is current says so with `aria-current="page"` rather than by being
unlinked. The links are buttons at the sizes the design language states, so a
row of them is the same height as the controls beside it.

        <PaginationNext href="#" />
      </PaginationItem>
    </PaginationContent>
  </Pagination>
</Preview>

The word on the previous and next links is hidden on a narrow viewport, where
the chevron alone is what fits.
