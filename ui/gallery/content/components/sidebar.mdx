A sidebar is a whole arrangement, not one component: `SidebarProvider` holds
whether it is open and the width the sidebar takes, `Sidebar` is the surface
itself, and `SidebarInset` is the content beside it. On a wide viewport the
sidebar is fixed to its edge and the inset takes the rest; on a narrow one it
collapses into a [Sheet](/components/sheet) that `SidebarTrigger` opens, and
`SidebarRail` drags.

The menu is built from `SidebarGroup`, `SidebarMenu`, `SidebarMenuItem` and
`SidebarMenuButton`: a button says whether it is the current one with `isActive`,
an action or a badge sits in its corner, and `SidebarMenuSub` is what its
children are listed in. `collapsible="icon"` keeps the icons while the labels go,
which is where the tooltips a button is given start being shown.

`variant="floating"` lifts the whole sidebar off the page and `variant="inset"`
puts the inset on the sidebar's own surface, which is the one that reads as a
single rounded frame around the content.
