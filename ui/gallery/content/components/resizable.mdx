---
title: Resizable
description: Panels whose sizes the reader sets.
---

import { ResizableHandle, ResizablePanel, ResizablePanelGroup } from "@trajs/shadcn/resizable";

A resizable group splits the space it is given, and each panel says how much of
it it takes: `defaultSize` is where it starts and `minSize` is where the handle
stops. `orientation` is the direction the split runs in, so a vertical group is
a column of panels rather than a row of them.

<Preview>
  <ResizablePanelGroup orientation="horizontal" className="h-48 overflow-hidden rounded-lg border">
    <ResizablePanel defaultSize="35%" minSize="20%">
      <div className="flex h-full items-center justify-center text-sm text-muted-foreground">
        sessions
      </div>
    </ResizablePanel>
    <ResizableHandle withHandle />
    <ResizablePanel defaultSize="65%">
      <ResizablePanelGroup orientation="vertical">
        <ResizablePanel defaultSize="60%">
          <div className="flex h-full items-center justify-center text-sm text-muted-foreground">
            conversation
          </div>
        </ResizablePanel>
        <ResizableHandle />
        <ResizablePanel defaultSize="40%">
          <div className="flex h-full items-center justify-center text-sm text-muted-foreground">
            tool payload
          </div>
        </ResizablePanel>
      </ResizablePanelGroup>
    </ResizablePanel>
  </ResizablePanelGroup>
</Preview>

`withHandle` draws the grip on the separator, which is what a reader has to see
before they try to drag it.
