A questionnaire is not a form: it is the same form restricted to one item at a
time, with the rest of the flow around it. The root declares every item and its
choices — which is what the keyboard shortcuts, the progress and the required
state are read from — and each item is written out again as the content it is
shown as.

`QuestionnaireProgress` states the position within the flow, `QuestionnaireItem`
is current only while it is the one being asked, and the actions move between
the items: `Previous`, `Skip` and `Next`, with `Submit` in the last one's place
when the item is the last. An answer that does not validate stops `Next` and is
read by `QuestionnaireError`.

The shortcuts are letters or numbers, stated once on the root, and are what the
small keys beside each choice are; an item with a single choice and an `Input`
is the one that is answered by typing.
