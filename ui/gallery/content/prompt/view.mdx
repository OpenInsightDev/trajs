---
title: PromptView
description: A whole prompt, as the conversation its messages make.
---

`PromptView` renders a `Prompt.Prompt` — every message it holds, in the order it
holds them — as one conversation. It is what a reader reaches for when they want
the exchange a trajectory recorded rather than the parts it is made of:
