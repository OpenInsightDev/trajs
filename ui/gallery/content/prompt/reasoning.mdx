---
title: PromptReasoning
description: What a model thought before it answered, kept out of the answer's way.
---

`Prompt.ReasoningPart` renders as `PromptReasoning`: the thinking folds away
behind a marker, because a thought belongs to the answer it was written for and
not to the person reading the answer. Open it to read it.

<Preview>
  <PromptReasoningDemo />
</Preview>

Thinking a response is still writing is the same component with `streaming` set:
it is open while it is being thought and folds away once it is written, and the
reader's own choice is what the panel follows from then on.
