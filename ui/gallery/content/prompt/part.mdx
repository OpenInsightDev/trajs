---
title: PromptPart
description: One part, rendered by the component its type maps to.
---

`PromptPart` takes a part that is known only as the union `effect/ai` gives it — a
part read out of a trajectory rather than one written out with its own type — and
renders it by the component its `type` maps to. Every kind of part a prompt
carries, through the one dispatch:

<Preview>
  <PromptPartDemo />
</Preview>

The dispatch reads a part's `type`, so a part `effect/ai` adds stops compiling
here until this package maps it, which is where a missing component should be
noticed.
