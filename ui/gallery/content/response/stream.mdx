---
title: ResponseStream
description: A response that is still arriving, read as the answer written so far.
---

`ResponseStream` reads the parts of a response that is still arriving as the
answer they have written. The increments of one chunk are folded into the part
they complete, so a text arrives as one bubble that grows rather than one bubble
per increment, and a chunk the stream has not closed is read in the streaming
state of the component its part maps to — which is what says the answer is not
finished.
