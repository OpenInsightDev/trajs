---
title: ResponseSource
description: What a model answered from — a URL or a document.
---

`Response.UrlSourcePart` and `Response.DocumentSourcePart` share one `type` and
are told apart by `sourceType`, and they share one component: `ResponseSource`.
A URL is the link it points at; a document is the reference a response holds, so
nothing about it is opened.
