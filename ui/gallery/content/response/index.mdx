---
title: Overview
description: The parts only a response carries, and the stream it arrives in.
---

A response is written with the same parts a prompt is, so its text, its
reasoning, its files and its tool calls are rendered by the [prompt
components](/prompt). What is left is the parts only a response carries —
`ResponseSource`, `ResponseMetadata` and `ResponseFinish` — and the stream it
arrives in.

`ResponsePart` dispatches over the whole response union the way
[`PromptPart`](/prompt/part) dispatches over a prompt's, and `ResponseStream`
reads the parts that have arrived as the answer they have written, with
`ResponseToolParams` and `ResponseError` for the two things a stream says that a
finished response does not.
