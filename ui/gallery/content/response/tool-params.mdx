---
title: ResponseToolParams
description: The parameters of a call that is still being written.
---

`Response.ToolParamsStartPart`, its deltas and its end are read by
`ResponseToolParams` as the parameters a model is writing. They are shown as the
fragments they are rather than as a payload — half a payload reads better as what
is being written — and the call they belong to arrives whole afterwards, read by
[`PromptToolCall`](/prompt/tool-call).
